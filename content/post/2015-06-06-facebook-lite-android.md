<h1 style="text-align: center;">What is Facebook Lite?</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">As the name suggests, Facebook's new app is a Lite version of the former app with some of the features disappeared, but faster, smaller and lighter in terms of data consumption.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It is made to work in the low network coverage areas across the developing countries of Asia and will also be launched in parts of Latin America, Africa and Europe over the next couple of weeks.</p>
<pre style="text-align: justify;">Read more about Google's response on this in form of <a href="/2015/06/google-search-lite" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Search Lite</a>.</pre>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Why Facebook Lite?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This app is designed to encourage users in the developing countries where mobile data services are limited as compared to the developed countries.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">"In many areas, networks can be slow and not able to support all the functionality found in Facebook for Android. Facebook Lite was built for these situations, giving people a reliable Facebook experience when bandwidth is at a minimum," <strong><a href="http://newsroom.fb.com/news/2015/06/announcing-facebook-lite/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vijay Shankar</a></strong>, Product Manager for Facebook Lite, wrote in a blog post.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Read <strong>Mark Zuckerberg's</strong> <a href="https://www.facebook.com/zuck/posts/10102163612609781" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">views</a> on Facebook Lite in his post yesterday.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: center;">Personal review for FB Lite</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Personally, I used it on Abdul's Micromax Canvas 2 Plus. I will like to mention a few points about Facebook Lite -</p>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">After installation, on its first turn, it took about whopping 17.26 seconds to initialize its first run. It ruined its first impression and my expectations about that app. But on second run, it became faster with no more than 2 seconds.</li>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">It is best to work with 2G internet network though.</li>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">At first, I expected that it will adapt to the internet speed. But it didn't; that is when I tried it on a WiFi network, it still displayed compressed images instead of HD.</li>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">As already said, if you are using Facebook Lite, forget about Videos, or Games.</li>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">At less than a MB in size, its worth a try, at least to experience a new data saving Facebook.</li>
</ul>
