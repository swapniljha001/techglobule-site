<p style="text-align: justify;">Today we are discussing about the <strong>Google Search Lite</strong>, the latest feature brought to you by <strong>Google Inc.</strong></p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">What is Google?</h2>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Google-Logo.jpg"><img class=" wp-image-877 size-medium alignright" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/Google-Logo-300x116.jpg" alt="Google Logo" width="300" height="116" /></a>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Google.com is basically a<b> </b><a title="Web search engine" href="https://www.google.co.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">search engine</a>, <a href="/category/android/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Android</a>, cloud computing, and various other software.<span style="font-size: 13.3333330154419px; line-height: 20px;"> </span>Most of its profits are derived from <a title="AdWords" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdWords">AdWords</a>,<span style="font-size: 13.3333330154419px; line-height: 20px;"> </span>which is basically online advertising service.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Google was founded by <a title="Larry Page" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Page">Larry Page</a> and <a title="Sergey Brin" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Brin">Sergey Brin</a> while they were Ph.D. students at <a title="Stanford University" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University">Stanford University</a>. Together they incorporated Google as a privately held company on September 4, 1998. <a href="http://www.google.co.in/about/company/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more...</a></p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">What is Google Search Lite?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Google recently announced that they will be launching a new faster interface known as "Search Lite" especially optimized for slower mobile internet connections.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It is made to work in the low network coverage areas across the developing countries and will be launched in parts of Brazil and India by the end of June 2015. Google has already tested the feature in Indonesia, which has networks very similar to India.</p>
<pre>Facebook has also launched a similar app Facebook Lite. <a href="/2015/06/facebook-lite-android/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more...
</a></pre>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">What's the need? How is it different from traditional Search?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Companies like Facebook and Google have been focusing on India which is expected to have an internet user base of 550 million by 2018. This is the next step of Google to increase its foothold in the Indian Markets after "<strong>Android One</strong>", under which it partnered with 3 Indian Handset Makers (Micromax, Karbonn, &amp; Spice) to make affordable Android smartphones available for a larger number of people.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This feature will transcode pages for quicker delivery whenever it will detect a slow connection. Google claims that the new feature will load pages <strong>4x faster</strong> and at the same time will consume <strong>80% lesser data</strong>.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Google stated that publishers will also benefit from this, as they will see a <strong>50% more</strong> page views. “Monetisation won’t be affected for publishers. There is an opt-out option for the users as well as publishers,” he said. “The feature is good for publishers as it will extend their reach,” he added.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Read <strong>Google’s</strong> <a href="https://plus.google.com/+google/posts/XZo6qgrNDE9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">views</a> on Google Search Lite on Google Plus.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How to use it?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Hiroto Tokusei</strong>, Product Manager, Google Inc, stated, “The new feature will come into play whenever a mobile user (will) be access search results from a very slow network. Google will capture the page and make it lighter.” He said, "With over 200 million Indians accessing the Internet from a smart phone, we’re working hard to make access faster and more affordable," He added in India, where 2G network connections are very slow, getting information on phone can be tough, and frustrating.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The service will be available to users browsing on Chrome and other Android browsers from Google search results.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Limitations</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">“Some stylesheets… having high image quality take more data on a slow network. These are compressed using the new (Search Lite) feature, and sometimes the page looks slightly different,” said Tokusei.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Although the users will have the option to view the original Web-page, but they would not be able to intentionally switch to Search Lite feature.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Google has not specified how 'slow' network will be considered slow enough for Search Lite to kick in automatically. So users don't really have an option to switch back and forth in Google Search and Search Lite. This might led to some criticism.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">Personal Review for Google Search Lite</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">According to me, the feature is very good as we have nothing to download or update, the feature will automatically switch on or off according to our network speed. This is something I expected from <a href="/2015/06/facebook-lite-android/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Facebook Lite</a>; but was disappointed.</p>
