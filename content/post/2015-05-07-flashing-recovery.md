<h2 style="text-align: center;" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">What is meant by a Recovery? Why should I use it?
</span></h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">Recovery is nothing but a medium or environment to change <strong>custom ROMs</strong> i.e. it helps the user to interactively change files in the operating system of a mobile device in an easier and convenient way. It can be compared to the <a href="http://i.imgur.com/Y9BZ197.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">BIOS</a> of a Windows PC.
</span></p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">How to flash a recovery?</span></h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">i) First and foremost, your device should be rooted before doing this. To know if your device is rooted or not, download <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joeykrim.rootcheck&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Root Checker</a> from the Google Play Store. To root your android device, you may prefer the <a href="/2015/03/ways-of-rooting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Ways of Rooting</a> post.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">ii) Now you need to find your device's recovery in your device's <a href="http://forum.xda-developers.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">forums.</a> Or else you will have to <a href="/2015/04/how-to-port-custom-recovery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">port</a> it yourself.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">iii) Download and install the app named <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobileuncle.toolbox&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mobile Uncle Tools</a> from Google Play Store.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">iv) Place your device's recovery in root folder of your SD Card. (Just to find it easily while using it).</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">v) Open Mobile Uncle Tools &gt; Recovery Update &gt; *.img</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">vi) Tap "Flash Recovery". After this step ,your device mayn't respond for a while, it's normal and nothing to worry.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">vii) You will see a message "Recovery flashed. Reboot into recovery mode". If you see this message, it means that you have successfully flashed the recovery image in your Android device.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="emojitext" data-reactid=".0.1:$main.$919650263989@c-us.1.$ml-919650263989@c-us.$msg-false_919650263989@c-us_1429281397-33.0.1.1.0.0">viii) Tap "Yes". You will be rebooted to your selected recovery. If you tap no, you will have to manually reboot into recovery mode.</span></p>
