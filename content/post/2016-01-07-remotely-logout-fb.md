<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">If you suspect that your hacker friend is secretly using your Facebook account without your knowledge, then you've come to the right article.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The easiest way to secure your <a href="https://www.facebook.com/techglobule/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Facebook</a> account is to <strong>log out </strong>from every logged in location and<strong> changing your password</strong> to a new one.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">STAGE 1 - How to remotely log out of Facebook?</h2>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/facebook.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-700 size-medium" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/facebook-300x113.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="113" /></a>
<ul>
    <li>Log in to your Facebook Account, on <a href="https://www.facebook.com/?_rdr=p" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">facebook.com</a>.
[caption id="attachment_1519" align="aligncenter" width="987"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Settings.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1519 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Settings.jpg" alt="Settings" width="987" height="627" /></a> Settings[/caption]
&nbsp;</li>
    <li>Click on More to reveal a menu.</li>
    <li>Go to Settings.
[caption id="attachment_1518" align="aligncenter" width="993"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Security.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1518 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Security.png" alt="Security Settings" width="993" height="655" /></a> Security Settings[/caption]
&nbsp;</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Go to Security tab &gt; Where You're Logged In &gt; End Activity. End all your activities.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Congratulations, you have successfully remotely logged out of Facebook from every place you forgot to log out earlier. <span class="_3oh-">??</span></li>
</ul>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">STAGE 2 - How to change my FB Password?</h2>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Log in to your Facebook Account, on <a href="https://www.facebook.com/?_rdr=p" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">facebook.com</a>.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Click on More to reveal a menu.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Go to Settings.
[caption id="attachment_1520" align="aligncenter" width="991"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/General.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1520 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/General.png" alt="General Settings" width="991" height="561" /></a> General Settings[/caption]</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Go to General tab &gt; Password &gt; New. Set and Type in a new password.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">In Re-type new, type your new password again.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Click on Save Changes.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Congratulations, you have successfully secured your Facebook account.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Like and share us on Facebook. <span class="_3oh-">??</span></li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Do give us feedback in comments below.</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>Note</strong> - These tasks can be accomplished from mobile devices as well, following the similar steps.</p>
