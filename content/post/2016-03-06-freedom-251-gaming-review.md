<p style="text-align: justify;">And here we have the Freedom 251 with us. Various people are doubting the gaming capabilities of this phone at the price of just 251 Indian Rupee. So we'll be doing the gaming review in this video.</p>
[embed]https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcNr3pJFMg4[/embed]
<p style="text-align: justify;">We did both heavy gaming as in Asphalt 8, and casual Gaming as in Subway Surfer.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.imangi.templerun&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Subway Surfers</a> is a casual endless runner game made by <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kiloo.subwaysurf&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Kiloo</a>. The touchscreen was quite responsive, which obeyed me without lagging at all.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Then, we tried <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gameloft.android.ANMP.GloftA8HM&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Asphalt 8</a>, made by Gameloft, which comes under the category of heavy gaming. Unlike what we were expecting, we experienced no lags at all, and the gameplay was all smooth.</p>

<pre>Play <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.TechyMauGameStudios.LittleGuardian" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Little Guardian</a> on your Android device.</pre>
The Freedom 251 smart-phone sports a 1.3GHz QuadCore processor which is quite better than a lot of phones out there. Even my <a href="/2015/06/yu-yuphoria-review/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">YU</a> <a href="/2015/06/yu-yuphoria-unboxing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Yuphoria</a> has only a 1.2 GHz Quad-Core processor.
<p style="text-align: justify;">It also sports 1GB of RAM which helps the case.</p>
