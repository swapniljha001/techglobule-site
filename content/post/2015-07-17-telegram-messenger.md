<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today we are discussing about the Telegram Messenger, an instant message carrier brought to you by Pavel Durov, who also brought <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VK_(social_networking_website)" rel="noopener">VK</a>.</p>
<h1 style="text-align: center;">What is Telegram?</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><b>Telegram</b> is an instant messaging system mainly focussed on privacy and multi-platform availability. Telegram clients exist for both mobile (<a href="/category/android" rel="noopener">Android</a>, <a href="/category/apple-ios/" rel="noopener">iOS</a>, <a href="/category/windows/windows-phone/" rel="noopener">Windows Phone</a>) and desktop systems (<a href="/category/windows/" rel="noopener">Windows</a>, OS X, <a href="/category/linux/" rel="noopener">Linux</a>).</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Telegram is run by a German nonprofit organization backed by entrepreneur and philanthropist <a title="Pavel Durov" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Durov">Pavel Durov</a>. Its client code is open-source software, while its server-side code is currently proprietary.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In short, Telegram is super fast, simple and way more secure than any of the messaging apps out there.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: justify;">Features -</h3>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
    <li><strong>Connect</strong> from most remote locations with even the connections.</li>
    <li><strong>Coordinate</strong> groups of up to 20,000 members.</li>
    <li><strong>Synchronize</strong> your chats across all your devices.</li>
    <li><strong>Send</strong> documents of any type and size.</li>
    <li><strong>Encrypt</strong> personal &amp; secret messages.</li>
    <li><strong>Destruct</strong> your messages with a timer.</li>
    <li><strong>Store</strong> your media in the cloud.</li>
    <li><strong>Build</strong> your own tools and bots on their API.</li>
</ul>
<h1 style="text-align: center;">Why use Telegram when we have Whatsapp?</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">You might be thinking that why should one even bother about any other messaging app when one can use <b>WhatsApp</b>, following is the list of differences which make Telegram better than any other messaging app out there including WhatsApp.</p>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It is <strong>cloud-based</strong>, which lets you access your messages from multiple devices.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It is <strong>open source.</strong></li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It is <strong>secure</strong>. Not only the messages are encrypted but Telegram also offers <strong>Secret chat</strong>, in which after a set interval of time, the messages are deleted from all the devices.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It is <strong>distributed</strong>. Its servers are spread worldwide to enhance speed and security.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It enables you to <strong>share</strong> unlimited high-resolution photos, videos, and files of any type up to sizes of 1.5 GB.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It has <strong>Bots</strong>. For e.g. Add @ImageBot in your contacts, whenever you message it something like /get Ferrari, it would reply with an image of Ferrari. Telegram supports many different kinds of bots for different tasks. You can even make a bot for yourself if you want to.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It supports @<strong>replies</strong>, mentions and #<strong>hashtags</strong>. No this is nothing complex. It makes Group Chatting easier. For e.g., if I want to reply to person A in a group of 100 people. I can type @A followed by my message, and only A will be notified.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It is <strong>free</strong> and will stay free forever.</li>
</ul>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">About the Developers</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">You can follow Telegram on <a href="https://twitter.com/telegram" rel="noopener">Twitter</a>, for ongoing news about it. Or check out its <a href="https://telegram.org/faq#q-will-you-have-ads-or-sell-my-data-or-steal-my-wife-and-enslave" rel="noopener">FAQ</a> section.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Telegram is supported by Pavel and Nikolai Durov.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Pavel Durov</strong> is known as the <strong>Mark Zuckerberg</strong> of Russia because his <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VK_(social_networking_website)" rel="noopener">VK</a> userbase outnumbered Facebook users in own own country that is Russia. Pavel supports Telegram financially and ideologically, while <b>Nikolai's</b> input is technological. To make Telegram possible, Nikolai developed a unique custom data protocol, which is open, secure and optimized for work with multiple data-centres. As a result, Telegram combines security, reliability and speed on any network.</p>
