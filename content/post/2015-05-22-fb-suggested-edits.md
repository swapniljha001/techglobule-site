<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, we are discussing how to make the <strong>World</strong> a better place to live in an easy manner with the help of <strong>technology</strong> and without you ever leaving your couch.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Why should I contribute in making World a better place?</h2>
Hmmmmmm.... You are seriously asking this question? Well, you live in this World and should try to make it better for your family, loved ones, kids, siblings, or at least&nbsp;for yourself.
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How can I make the World a better place through technology?</h2>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/facebook.jpg"><img class="alignright wp-image-700 size-medium" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/facebook-300x113.jpg" alt="facebook" width="300" height="113"/></a>
<p style="text-align: justify;">I'm assuming that you are familiar with <a href="http://facebook.com/techglobule" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Facebook&nbsp;</a> and have a Facebook Account. Facebook, not only provide services to chat, post status, and like, and comment and share but also has a hidden feature which helps make the World a better place by allowing its user to suggest some edits to places, pages, or anything else.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This means that you can simply verify if the given single statement about a specific place, news, or page etc. is true or not by personal experience. If your suggested edit is accepted, it would help a lot of other people who are searching that specific place will get updated and better results only because of <strong>You</strong>.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It is really an easy way to make the better place without much efforts. All you have to do is choose from <strong>Yes</strong>, <strong>No</strong>, or <strong>Skip</strong> for a given question.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">So, next time you have some leisure period open your Facebook app or site, and find <strong>Suggested Edits</strong> in the Apps.</p>
