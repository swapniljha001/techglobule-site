<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
Today we are gonna discuss about how to change the Device's ID, Android ID, SIM serial number and subscriber ID of any Android device.
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Why should I change? Are there any benefits?</h2>
It has many advantages. Foremost benefit you can make your friends jealous by showing Android <strong>5.5</strong> in <strong>About Phone</strong> settings whereas in reality, it will be a lot lower.
Now practical benefits include, using <strong>coupon codes</strong>, many a times you can't use a certain coupon code on your device just because you or someone else used a similar type of coupon code on your phone. This problem is caused because many apps such as <strong>PayTM</strong>, <strong>SnapDeal</strong> etc save your device's ID and don't let you earn from those hard-earned coupon of yours.
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How to do so?</h2>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">Prerequisites</h3>
First of all you will need a Rooted android device. To check if it's rooted or not, install <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joeykrim.rootcheck&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Root Checker</a>. If it isn't rooted, check our <a title="Ways of Rooting" href="/2015/03/ways-of-rooting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">How to root</a> post. If you have a rooted Android device, you will need the following apps to change your device's ID and other such things.
<ul>
    <li style="color: #666666; font-family: 'Trebuchet MS', Trebuchet, Verdana, sans-serif; line-height: 20px;"><a title="Xposed Framework" href="/2015/04/xposed-framework/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Xposed Framework</a></li>
    <li style="color: #666666; font-family: 'Trebuchet MS', Trebuchet, Verdana, sans-serif; line-height: 20px;"><a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=eu.donkeyguard&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Donkey Guard App</a></li>
    <li style="color: #666666; font-family: 'Trebuchet MS', Trebuchet, Verdana, sans-serif; line-height: 20px;"><a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amphoras.hidemyroot&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">HideMyRoot App</a></li>
    <li style="color: #666666; font-family: 'Trebuchet MS', Trebuchet, Verdana, sans-serif; line-height: 20px;"><a href="http://repo.xposed.info/module/com.devadvance.rootcloak2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RootCloak App</a></li>
    <li><a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vivek.imeichanger&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IMEI Changer App</a></li>
</ul>
All these apps add upto no more than 13.1 MB.
After downloading the above required apps. Follow these steps -
1. Launch the Xposed Framework, go to the "Framework" section and click on "Install/Update" &gt; Reboot.
<ol>
<li>Install IMEI Changer app and change the IMEI of your device.
Note - But remember your original IMEI number for when any problem occurs.</li>
<li><p>In Xposed Framework App, go to modules section &gt; enable Donkey Guard App and RootCloak App in it.</p></li>
<li><p>If you want a certain app, say Snapdeal to not know your root status. Open RootClock app  &gt; click on "Add/Remove Apps" &gt; click on the plus sign on top right (to add any app) &gt; Add Snapdeal from it. Similarly, you can add any app to this list.</p></li>
<li><p>After doing the above, open the HideMyRoot App and click on "Hide SU Binaries".</p></li>
<li><p>Final step, now open Donkey Guard App and change "<strong>Android ID</strong>, <strong>Device ID</strong>, <strong>SIM Serial No.</strong> , <strong>Subscriber ID</strong>" (You can click on the pencil icon to generate new values automatically).</p></li>
<li><p>Reboot your device, and its done.</p></li>
</ol>
