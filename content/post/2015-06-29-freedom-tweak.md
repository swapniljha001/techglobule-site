<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, we are gonna learn how to get&nbsp;In-App purchases for free in any <strong>jailbroken</strong>&nbsp;iDevice&nbsp;by using&nbsp;this famous or I may say&nbsp;infamous tweak called <strong>LocalIAPStore</strong>.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Why a post on getting in app purchases for free?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">TechGlobule by no means is supporting and will never support Piracy. LocalIAPStore is meant for fun purposes and making gameplay easier for various different apps.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How does the app works?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Freedom acts as an alternative wallet, featuring payments while in-app purchases in almost all the apps.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">Who’s the developer? and List of Supported Apps</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">You can research a little bit about the developers behind Freedom on its&nbsp;official <a href="http://in-appstore.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">website</a>. The nicknames of developers are ZonD80 and MadKite. You can follow ZonD80 on <a href="https://twitter.com/zond80" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Twitter</a>, if you know&nbsp;<strong>Russian</strong>.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">LocalIAPStore has published a list of supported apps on their <a href="https://locallapstore.wordpress.com/list-of-working-games-with-locallapstore/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">blog</a>; although it is incomplete. You can also help in completing that list.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Will it support my device?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The app supports all jailbroken iDevices running iOS 6&nbsp;to iOS 8.1. It worked fine on iPhone 4S (<strong>iOS 8.1.2</strong>) and iPhone 5&nbsp;(<strong>iOS 7.1.2</strong>).</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">In which Repo will I find it?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It is available for <strong>free</strong> on the In-AppStore.com Cydia Repository in Cydia. Simply go to Sources &gt; In-AppStore.com Cydia Repository &gt; All Packages &gt; LocalIAPStore &gt; Install &gt; Confirm&nbsp;and he tweak will be installed. If you are unsure how to install, see our <a href="/2015/04/cydia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">post</a>&nbsp;or <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1IeTiRvkc9k" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">YouTube video</a> to get assistance regarding installations in Cydia.&nbsp;In-AppStore.com Cydia Repo - <em>http://system.in-appstore.com/repo/</em></p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How to use it?</h2>
<h5 style="text-align: justify;">Method 1 (Standard Receipt). Follow these steps –</h5>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
	<li>After installing the tweak, go to Settings &gt; LocalIAPStore.</li>
	<li>Enable the first option and disable "Generate custom receipts".</li>
	<li>Open any app featuring in-app purchase.</li>
	<li>Try to buy something, a pop-up will be displayed&nbsp;asking for your Apple ID's password. Tap Cancel.</li>
	<li>{Optional step}. If the pop-up appears again and again, tap on Cancel only. Sometimes the pop-up appears up to 5 times. But saving a dollar is worth wasting a minute.</li>
	<li>You are done. You will get whatever you tried to bought.</li>
</ul>
<h5 style="text-align: justify;">Method 2 (Custom Receipt). Follow these steps -</h5>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">After installing the tweak, go to Settings &gt; iTunes &amp; App Store &gt; Apple ID:abcd@efgh.com.</li>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Tap on your Apple ID, a pop-up will be displayed with options, View Apple ID, Sign Out, iForgot, and Cancel. Choose Sign out.</li>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Then go to Settings &gt; LocalIAPStore. Enable both the options.</li>
	<li>Open any app featuring in-app purchase.</li>
	<li>Try to buy something, a pop-up will be displayed. Tap Buy, then on Apple ID pop-up, tap Cancel.</li>
	<li>You are done. You will get whatever you tried to purchase.</li>
</ul>
Enjoy!! It works with an internet connection to work.
