<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
Today we are providing a list of some great tweaks we found this month.
<h2 style="text-align: center;">List of Cydia Tweaks we found in May 2015</h2>
<ul>
	<li>
<h4>AppETA</h4>
It helps a user to know the Estimated Time of Arrival of any downloading app.
It costs <strong>0.99 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>Applocker</h4>
As the name suggests, it will lock specified apps with a pass code.
It costs <strong>0.99 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on ModMyi.com Repo.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li>
<h4>Barrel</h4>
It will add some great graphics to your iDevice icons.
It costs <strong>2.99 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>CacheClearer</h4>
It is used to Reset any app's cache.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on RPetrich Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>Cask</h4>
It adds some great graphics to your scrolling.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on RPtetrich Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>CurtiansForYou</h4>
It adds an attractive back gesture.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on RPetrich Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>DetailedBatteryUsage</h4>
It adds a usage Graph to Battery usage.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>Iconoclasm</h4>
It is used to customize icon grids.
It costs <strong>3.00 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on ModMyi.com Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>MIUI V5 Port</h4>
This is a MIUI 5 inspired theme.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on ZodTTD &amp; MacCiti Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>NoSlowAnimations</h4>
This tweak will help you speed up iOS's default fade animation.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
</ul>
We will discuss these tweaks one by one in future posts.
