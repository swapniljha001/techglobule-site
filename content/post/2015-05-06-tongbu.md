<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, we are gonna learn how to get paid apps for free in an iOS device without jailbreak or use of PC, this famous or I may say infamous app is called <strong>Tongbu</strong>.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Why a post on Downloading Paid Apps for free?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">TechGlobule by no means is supporting or will ever support Piracy. We are with all our might against Piracy.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">And the only reason we are showcasing Tongbu, because it is a way to users who are willing to pay for an app or game, but want to try it first before paying their hard-earned money. Many of the times if not all, after buying an app, it seems unworthy of the money spent.</p>
<pre style="text-align: justify;">Want some more apps for free. Try <a href="/2015/07/kuaiyong/">Kuaiyong</a> instead.</pre>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Who is the developer?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Tongbu</strong>, which means “<strong>sync</strong>” in Chinese, was a forum for cracked apps that has spawned its own tool for downloading pirated apps called Sync, i.e. <a href="https://www.facebook.com/Tongbu.official" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tongbu</a>. It's been made by the Chinese, and we should thank them for this app.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How does the app works?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Tongbu acts as an alternative App Store, featuring almost all the apps on the original App Store.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The only difference is that all the apps are free on Tongbu.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">Will it support my device?</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Yes, the app supports all iPhones and iPads, whether jail-broken or not. I have not tested it on an iPod, but I have a hunch it might work on it as well. It worked fine on iOS 7.x.x. and iOS 9.x.x. You will have to test yourself if you are using any other iOS version. Don't worry, even if the test fails, the worst thing that can happen is that you will not be able to open the Tongbu app after installing it, in such a case you can simply delete it. I personally tested it on an iPhone 4 (<strong>iOS 7.1.2</strong>) and iPad 2 (<strong>iOS 8.1.3</strong>, now<strong> iOS 9.0</strong>) and iPhone 5 (<strong>iOS 9.0</strong>).</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">How to get it?</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">i) Open <strong>Safari</strong> in the iOS device. This only works on Safari.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">ii) Open <a href="http://tongbu.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tongbu</a>. This link will redirect you appropriately according to your device and iOS version.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">iii) You will land a site in Chinese language. It will have 2 buttons, one with an <strong>Apple Logo</strong> (that's for non - jailbroken devices), and one with a<strong> PineApple Logo</strong> (that's for jailbroken devices)<strong>.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">iv) Tap on the button according to your device &gt; Install. In case you don't know if your device is jailbroken or not, you can simply tap the <strong>Apple Logo</strong> button, it works on both jailbroken as well as non - jailbroken devices.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">v) A box will appear asking tbsq.tongbu.com would like to install “@!#3.3.0”. Tap on Install.
Here, @!# represents Tongbu written in Chinese, and 3.3.0 is Tongbu version. You can install any version, whatever is available to your iDevice as per your iOS version.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">vi) For <strong>iOS 9</strong>: Go to Settings &gt; General &gt; Profiles &gt; Fuzhou Xiao Yue Trade Co. &gt; <strong>Trust</strong>.
For <strong>iOS 8 or below</strong>: When opening the app, a message may occur asking you if you trust the app developer. Tap on <strong>Trust</strong>.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">How to use it?</h3>
<h4>For iPhone users -</h4>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">There is a slight catch in Tongbu's iPhone version. It's in Chinese.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Don't despair yet.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It's still very easy to use.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Refrain using Apple ID in the Tongbu app.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It will prompt a message to install 12-15 sponsored apps. You can easily cancel that by using the above cancel button present in the top left corner of screen.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">No need to configure its settings, the default settings are well enough.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You will see a search bar in the top.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Search for any app, let's say Need For Speed.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You will find a list of apps with icons to their right, if the icon is circle with down arrow, you can easily download and run that app.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">These icons keep changing as new updates come for these apps. If you find an unfamiliar icon in front of an app, you may not be able to download it.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">For <strong>iOS 9</strong>: Go to Settings &gt; General &gt; Profiles &gt; 'something 'Co. &gt; <strong>Trust</strong>.
For <strong>iOS 8 or below</strong>: When opening the app, a message may occur asking you if you trust the app developer. Tap on <strong>Trust</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<h4>For iPad users -</h4>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Congrats! most probably you got an English version of Tongbu.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You don't need any instructions, you can freely explore the app and its settings. But in case, you got a Chinese version of the app and you need instructions follow the same instructions given for iPhone above.</li>
</ul>
<h4>After downloading the app -</h4>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">The tongbu app will ask for a permission to install the app. Tap on yes.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">On opening the newly installed app, it will ask the same trust question which you encountered while opening Tongbu. Tap on Trust.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You can see Downloads by tapping the "arrow in box" button on right side of search bar.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">The first screen shows currently downloading apps. Swipe from right to left to go to second screen.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">The second one shows downloaded apps. Swipe from left to right to go to first screen.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You can tap on any downloaded app to reveal 2 options, the blue option is for installing that game, and the red option is to delete that downloaded file after installing it.</li>
</ul>
<h4 style="text-align: center;">Note for Jailbroken users</h4>
Jailbroken users can also use the same way to download free Tongbu.
