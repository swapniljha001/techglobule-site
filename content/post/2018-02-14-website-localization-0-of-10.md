<h2 style="text-align: center;">What is Website Localization?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Website localization</strong> is a fancy way of referring to the process of <strong>adapting</strong> a particular website to a <strong>local language</strong> to serve the <strong>demographic people</strong> of a particular country or place better.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: center;">Why do I need it? How is it different from simple translation?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It's a bit different from simple translation, as it adapts to the language while maintaining the cultural context of the content. In other words, my lame jokes will be properly translated or replaced with equally lame jokes from a different language. Thus, maintaining the Law of Conservation of Lameness.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">We at <strong><a href="" rel="noopener">TechGlobule</a></strong>, just use a Google Translate Widget for this, which more often than not, has erroneous translations.</p>
