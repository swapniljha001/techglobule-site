<h2 style="text-align: center;">How does it work?</h2>
[caption id="attachment_1791" align="alignright" width="300"]<a href="/2017/01/vshare-ios/03-vshare-install/#main" rel="attachment wp-att-1791"><img class="size-medium wp-image-1791" src="/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/03-vShare-Install-300x207.png" alt="vShare Helper Installer" width="300" height="207" /></a> vShare Helper Installer[/caption]
<strong>vShare</strong> acts as an alternative to <strong>App Store</strong>, featuring all the apps on the original App Store and more. It (mis)uses the Developer certificates issued by Apple.
Apple issues Developer certificates to companies which want all of their employees to have a specific app. vShare uses this certificate and pretend that all its users are its employees. So you see, the apps you’ll install are genuine.
The only difference is that all the apps are free.
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Extra Benefits</h2>
<ul>

    <li style="text-align: justify;">You can install apps not available in your country's app store.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You can install apps which are no longer supported by the App Store, or discontinued.
I played FIFA 14 as well, because it played more smoothly on an old iPad 2.</li>

<h2 style="text-align: center;">How to get it?</h2>
You can just follow the screenshots steps, down below, or you can read the steps, :)
<ol>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Open a Windows PC/Laptop, go to <strong><a href="http://www.vshare.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">vShare.com</a></strong> and download vShare for PC using the <strong>Download</strong> button.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Once downloaded, open the file to install it.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Choose <strong>Yes</strong> in Windows User Control dialogue box, if it appears. Refer to screenshots.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Choose <strong>one key installation</strong> button, in vShare Helper installation.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">After it is installed, click on <strong>Try it Now</strong>.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Click the <strong>X</strong> button to dismiss paid features.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">If any Apple Driver is missing, you will get this Fail screen, just use the <strong>Download</strong> button.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">vShare will reboot automatically, and then connect your iOS Device.
For e.g. an old <strong>iPad 2</strong> in my case.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">A dialogue box will appear scanning Apple Drivers, on your Laptop, ignore it. But tap on <strong>Trust</strong> on your iOS Device.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Click on <strong>Install vShare</strong>.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You will find vShare install in your iOS Device in a couple of moments.</li>
</ol>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How to use it?</h2>
<h4>Beginning -</h4>
<ol>
    <li>Open the app, skip the advertised apps to prevent installing them.</li>
    <li>Search for any app in top most search bar, e.g. <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/in/app/monopoly-for-ipad/id405634168?mt=8&amp;ign-mpt=uo%3D2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Monopoly</a>.</li>
    <li>Tap on <strong>Get</strong> button. It might shoot an advertisement at you, and you will land in original <strong>App Store</strong>. Go back to <strong>vShare</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<h4>Downloading -</h4>
<ol>

    <li>Well, try to keep vShare in the foreground till installation is complete.
<ol>
    <li>Now, just run the app and enjoy.</li>
    <li>If you can;t run the app,
Go to Settings &gt; General &gt; Profiles &gt; ‘something’ Group Co. &gt; <strong>Trust</strong>. Then run the app and enjoy.</li>
</ol>

</ol>
