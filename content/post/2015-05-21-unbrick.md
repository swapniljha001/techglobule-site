<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today we are discussing how to unbrick any Android device with MTK (<strong><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MediaTek" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MediaTek</a></strong>) chip set. We are here talking about every Android device, but I will use the word Phone for ease of writing.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">What is brick?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Getting Bricked is a state when your phone does not boot up (that means it doesn't start) and gets stuck in a boot loop due to erroneous, incomplete or corrupted system files. Your smart phone is no more smart and is as useful as a <a href="http://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brick_wall_close-up_view.jpg#/media/File:Brick_wall_close-up_view.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">brick</a>.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How can my smartphone get bricked?</h2>
Your smart phone can get bricked while <a href="/2015/03/ways-of-rooting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Rooting</a> it, or <a href="/2015/05/flashing-recovery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">flashing</a> a <a href="/category/android/custom-roms/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Custom ROM</a>, or <a href="/2015/04/how-to-port-custom-recovery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">porting</a> a Custom Recovery in it. It is a problem which can be faced by any person by accidental deletion of any important system file.
Or it can also be caused by flashing or porting any damaged file.
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How can I unbrick my phone?</h2>
[caption id="attachment_692" align="aligncenter" width="300"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/bricked.jpg"><img class="size-medium wp-image-692" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/bricked-300x300.jpg" alt="Bricked" width="300" height="300" /></a> Bricked Android[/caption]
<h3 style="text-align: justify;">Material Required -</h3>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
	<li>A <strong>working PC</strong>.</li>
	<li>SmartPhone Flash Tools (<strong>SP Flash Tools</strong>).
SP Flash Tools is created by MediaTek Inc. and is a trusted software.
The following link will download the latest version of SP Flash Tools as on date 21 May 2015.
<a href="http://www.mediafire.com/download/5f5h3owa7s7y6g2/SP-Flash-Tool-v5.1352.01.zip" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Download it</a>!</li>
</ul>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
	<li><strong>Android USB Drivers</strong>
These are needed to operate the Android device via your PC.
The following link will help you download the latest version of ADB Drivers as on date 21 May 2015.
<a href="http://xdafirmware.com/drivers/ADB-Driver/ADB-Driver-v1.4.2.zip" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Download it!</a></li>
</ul>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
	<li>A <strong>ROM</strong>.
A ROM will be required to flash and bring back to device to life. But remember flashing a Stock ROM (that originally came with the phone) will also help in getting your phone's guarantee back; whereas flashing a Custom ROM (any other ROM) has a lesser chance of getting your phone unbricked and has nothing to do with its guarantee.
You can find it on your device's <a href="http://www.xda-developers.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">forums</a>.</li>
</ul>
<h3 style="text-align: justify;">Steps to unbrick your phone -</h3>
[caption id="attachment_693" align="alignright" width="300"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/SP1.png"><img class="size-medium wp-image-693" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/SP1-300x188.png" alt="SP Tools 1" width="300" height="188" /></a> SP Tools 1[/caption]
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Charge your smart phone to approximately 50 percent or above battery level.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Switch it off and remove its battery, if possible.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Install SP Flash Tools and Android USB Drivers. Download them from above link given in their description.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Extract the stock ROM/ backup ROM from .zip file. Place the extracted folder to the desktop for ease of access.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Open the Device manager in PC. Go to Start &gt; Run &gt; <strong>devmgmt.msc.</strong></li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Connect your phone to PC through data cable.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">In the device manager, a yellow line would have appeared after the phone got connected. <strong>MT65xx preloader</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Right click on it and click <strong>Update</strong>. Browse to Desktop and click <strong>Next</strong>. A red box will appear, press <strong>Continue Installation.</strong></li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Open SP Flash tool folder and click on <strong>Flash_Tool.exe</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Browse to the scatter File <strong>MT65xx_Android_scatter_emmc_PMT.txt</strong> in the folder of Stock ROM, which was earlier placed on Desktop.
[caption id="attachment_694" align="alignright" width="300"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/sp2.png"><img class="size-medium wp-image-694" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/sp2-300x218.png" alt="SP Tools 2" width="300" height="218" /></a> SP Tools 2[/caption]</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Make sure all the boxes are checked except <strong>USRDATA</strong>. Then press the <strong>Download</strong> button.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Then re - connect your mobile phone. You will notice some progress  on the CPU screen in the form of blue and red colour.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">After a while, you'll get a Green circle.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Disconnect your phone. Plug in the battery and switch it on.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">Enjoy your New and Alive smart phone.</li>
</ul>
<h4 style="text-align: center;">Important Note, in case of failure -</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">During this process if you get any error, then try everything again, but this time, instead of using <strong>Download</strong> button use <strong>Firmware Upgrade</strong> button. Check all the files including <strong>USRDATA</strong>. Your Rom will get flashed. For any other errors you can ask us in the comments below.</p>
