<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">If you suspect that your hacker friend is secretly using your Google account (which can range from <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCgoH7cm0fv-0K0PyQXUR-Bg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">YouTube</a> to a large number of different services by <a href="https://plus.google.com/+TechGlobule" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google</a>) without your knowledge, then you've come to the right article.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The easiest way to secure your Google account is to <strong>log out </strong>from every logged in location and<strong> changing your password</strong> to a new one.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">If anyone's getting confused, GMail Account is equivalent to Google Account.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">STAGE 1 - How to remotely log out of Google?</h2>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Log in to your Google Account on <a href="http://mail.google.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">mail.google.com</a>.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Scroll down the page.
[caption id="attachment_1548" align="aligncenter" width="1351"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Account-Activity-e1452240460241.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1548 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Account-Activity-e1452240460241.png" alt="Account Activity" width="1351" height="510" /></a> Details[/caption]</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Click on Details near below Last Account Activity.
[caption id="attachment_1545" align="aligncenter" width="758"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Logout.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1545 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Logout.png" alt="Remote Logout" width="758" height="138" /></a> Remote Logout[/caption]</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">A new window will open with a button of Sign out all other web session. Click on it.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Congratulations, you have successfully remotely logged out of Facebook from every place you forgot to log out earlier. <span class="_3oh-"><img class="emoji" draggable="false" src="http://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/72x72/1f61b.png" alt="?" /><img class="emoji" draggable="false" src="http://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/72x72/1f61b.png" alt="?" /></span></li>
</ul>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">STAGE 2 - How to change my Google Password?</h2>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Log in to your Google Account on <a href="http://mail.google.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">mail.google.com</a>.
[caption id="attachment_1547" align="aligncenter" width="1350"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Settings.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1547 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Settings.png" alt="GMail Settings" width="1350" height="660" /></a> GMail Settings[/caption]</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Click on the Gear icon to reveal a menu.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Go to Settings.
[caption id="attachment_1549" align="aligncenter" width="1330"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Change-Pass.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1549 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Change-Pass.png" alt="Change Password" width="1330" height="571" /></a> Change Pass[/caption]</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Go to Account and Import tab &gt; Change Password.
[caption id="attachment_1544" align="aligncenter" width="195"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Login.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1544 size-medium" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Login-195x300.png" alt="Google Login" width="195" height="300" /></a> Google Login[/caption]</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You will redirect to a page asking you to login again. (This is a just a security measure, nothing to worry about)
[caption id="attachment_1546" align="aligncenter" width="1350"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Pass.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-1546 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/Pass.png" alt="New Password" width="1350" height="656" /></a> New Password[/caption]</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">You will this page. Just type in your new password in both the columns. Click on Save Password.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Follow us on Google+.<span class="_3oh-"> <img class="emoji" draggable="false" src="http://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/72x72/1f61b.png" alt="?" /><img class="emoji" draggable="false" src="http://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/72x72/1f61b.png" alt="?" /></span></li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Do give us feedback in comments below.</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>Note</strong> - These tasks can be accomplished from mobile devices as well, following the similar steps.</p>
