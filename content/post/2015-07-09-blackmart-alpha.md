<h2 style="text-align: center;">What is BlackMart Alpha?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="black">BlackMart Alpha is an excellent substitute for the Google Play Store, because not only it can get you an app for free, but also it offers its market in multi-language selection which will attract any Android user of any country.</span></p>
<h3 style="text-align: justify;">Features -</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span class="black">BlackMart Alpha also has the following features. that makes it easy to use :</span></p>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;"><span class="black">Multilingual option for a global user experience.</span></li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;"><span class="black">Completely free, without the need for any payment methods.</span></li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It also offers apps that are not yet officially released in one's country.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It also provides downloading of different versions of the same app.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It helps you update your apps before they are officially updated in your country.</li>
</ul>
<h2 style="text-align: left;">How To Get BlackMart Alpha?</h2>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;"><span class="black">Download the latest version (as on date) of BlackMart Alpha from this link of <a href="http://blackmart.us/blackmart_apk.apk">BlackMart.us.</a></span></li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Go to Settings &gt; Security &gt; Unknown Sources. Make sure that Unknown Sources is checked.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Install the BlackMart App.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Launch the app, you will find a magnifying glass icon in the top right corner.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Tap on the icon, and type the app's name you want.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Select the app you want to download from the search results.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Tap on Install, on the top right corner. You may be asked to one from  2 or more different versions of the same app.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Downloading will start. After downloading, it will ask you to install the app.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">After installation of the app, enjoy.</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Note - </strong>For bigger apps (usually more than 100MB), BlackMart Alpha will download the initial file (small-sized) and install it. When you will launch that app for the first time, it will download the rest.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Limitations</h2>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">It will display some ads in between downloading and installing other apps.</li>
</ul>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
    <li>Unlike Google Play Store, it fails to show compatible apps only, Thus, it is possible that you will not be able to run some of the apps you downloaded.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">To prevent the second limitation, you can try to install a lower version of that app. And if needed, update the app from within.</li>
    <li style="text-align: justify;">Any app uploaded to BlackMart is instantly available for download without any moderation or testing. Thus, some of the apps may prove harmful to download.</li>
</ul>
