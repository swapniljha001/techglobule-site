<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, we will discuss how to upgrade your current <a href="/category/android/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Android</a> version to Android MarshMallow in Samsung Galaxy S4 GT-i9500.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;"><b>Why Android MarshMallow?</b></h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/MarshMallow.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1560 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/MarshMallow.jpg" alt="Android MarshMallow" width="1280" height="853" /></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The reason I like Android Operating System are the increasing customizability options in the Android updates. It is also the reason I and many other users have to find some ways to get Android update when our device’s vendors are not in likely mood to do so.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, I am focussing light on a way to get Android Marshmallow 6.0.1 in Samsung Galaxy S4 GT-i9500. I’m showcasing this article because of many different reasons. Foremost of which is, I myself own this device, and I would love to use MarshMallow on my Galaxy S4.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">About Custom ROM (CM 13)<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/cm2.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-404 size-full" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/cm2.jpg" alt="CyanogenMod" width="600" height="200" /></a></h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">First of all, I would like to specify that Custom ROM and Custom firmware are one of the same thing. Also I may refer Samsung Galaxy S4 GT-i9500 as i9500.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Android MarshMallow 6.0.1 is now available to install on Samsung Galaxy s4 GT-i9500 via the <a href="/tag/cyanogenmod/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CyanogenMod</a> 13, Custom ROM. This CyanogenMod 13 custom firmware is an unofficial release which is based on Android 6.0.1 and comes with various new and different features and customization tools that resemble Android Open Source Project (<a href="https://source.android.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AOSP</a>) ROM. But as this update/firmware has been developed by third-party developers, and not officially released by Samsung, it is still in beta and there may be quite some bugs, and it is possible that maybe a feature or 2 may be buggy or even not work at all.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">About the Developer</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The developer behind this project goes by the alias of <a href="https://github.com/Alberto96" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Alberto96</a> and belongs to Italy. So far they have been able to get the video, audio, RIL and many other things working perfectly fine. Sadly, the <strong>stock camera app</strong> is not working; which means some users (not all) might face a problem. As the developer has been working on the build and is fixing bugs every now and then, I wouldn’t be surprised if those broken/buggy issues have been fixed by the time you finish reading and sharing this post.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">Bugs and Workarounds</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">As the stock camera app is still buggy, just download any other camera app from the Google <a href="https://play.google.com/store" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Play Store</a> to use the camera, for e.g. <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.GoogleCamera" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Camera</a>.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;"><strong>How to Update GT-i9500 to Android 6.0.1 MarshMallow via CyanogenMod 13 Custom Firmware</strong></h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Follow these steps (without cheating <a class="emoji" title="U+1F602" href="http://apps.timwhitlock.info/emoji/tables/unicode#emoji-modal"><span class="emoji">?</span></a><a class="emoji" title="U+1F602" href="http://apps.timwhitlock.info/emoji/tables/unicode#emoji-modal"><span class="emoji">?</span></a>) -</p>
[caption id="" align="aligncenter" width="3264"]<a href="http://i.imgur.com/WLOzbXQ.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img src="http://i.imgur.com/WLOzbXQ.jpg" alt="Samsung Galaxy S4" width="3264" height="2448" /></a> Samsung Galaxy S4[/caption]
<ol style="text-align: justify;">
    <li>You need to have these files on a computer. So, download the ROM (279.6MB) from this <a href="https://www.androidfilehost.com/?fid=24438995911969919" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">link</a></li>
    <li>Now, you also need to download the Google Apps Package (called GApps) from the site <a href="http://opengapps.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">OpenGApps.org.</a> Choose options -
Platform : ARM
Android : 6.0
Variant : micro
Download the package with the circular red download button below the form.</li>
    <li>Save both files on your desktop. Don’t unzip them.</li>
    <li>Connect your i9500 with your computer with the help of a USB cable.</li>
    <li>Copy those zipped files to your phone’s SD card.</li>
    <li>When the transfer is completed, unplug the USB cable, switch off your i9500 by holding the power button for a few seconds.<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/CM.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-402 size-medium" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/CM-300x300.jpg" alt="CyanogenMod" width="300" height="300" /></a></li>
    <li>After switching it off, wait for a few seconds. *think about the pollution and plan on planting a tree*</li>
    <li>Now, press the Home button, Power button, and Volume Up button simultaneously to boot the device in Recovery mode.</li>
    <li>Once in recovery mode, use the Volume up/Volume Down buttons to scroll up/down and Power button to select an option.</li>
    <li>First choose “wipe data factory reset” followed by “wipe cache partition” and “wipe dalvik cache”.</li>
    <li>Then, from the same, main menu of recovery select “install zip from SD card” and “choose zip from SD card”.</li>
    <li>Pick the Marshmallow firmware file and install CM13 Custom ROM in your Samsung Galaxy S4. Now CM13, is flashed in your device.</li>
    <li>Repeat the same steps to flash the GApps package too.</li>
    <li>After installation, reboot device, it may take up to 10 minutes to boot. There's nothing to worry about.</li>
    <li>It will boot up with CyanogenMod 13, which is based on MarshMallow 6.0.1.</li>
</ol>
