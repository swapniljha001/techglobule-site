<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today we are gonna discuss about a common Benchmarking tool which is widely used to benchmark phones and devices based on the <a title="ARM architecture" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARM_architecture">ARM architecture</a>, known as the <strong>Antutu Benchmark</strong>.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">What is Benchmark?</h2>
<div>With such rapid development in world of mobile devices, it is getting complicated for users to choose a dream device of theirs. Here comes, a Benchmarking app in vision, it will help a user to benchmark his or her device and get a specific score which one can use to compare his device to a variety of different devices in spite of many differences in both the devices, like different software, hardware, operating device etc.</div>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">Why is Antutu Benchmark better?</h2>
<div>
[caption width="200" id="attachment_488" align="alignright"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/2015-04-20-20.02.51.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-488 size-medium" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/2015-04-20-20.02.51-200x300.png" alt="Antutu 5" width="200" height="300"/></a> Antutu 5[/caption]The benchmark applications Antutu Benchmark gives a device a very precise and accurate score on the basis of its performance in&nbsp; a variety of tests. It gives it score in every test. And adds the sum total of all tests to give the device its final score.
</div>
<div></div>
<div>Unlike other benchmarks, it provides the user to choice to customize the number of test to run.</div>
<div></div>
<div>Did we mentioned, it's free to use...</div>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">It tests the device by testing the following -</h3>
<h4 style="text-align: justify;">UX (Multitask, Runtime)</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">UX means the test of <strong>User Experience</strong> of the Device.</p>
<h4 style="text-align: justify;">CPU</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">CPU means the tests of <strong>Central Processing Unit</strong> of the device.</p>
<h4 style="text-align: justify;">GPU</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">GPU means the test of <strong>Graphics Processing Unit</strong> of the device. The graphics like screen resolution and gaming graphics (both 2D and 3D Graphics) are tested.</p>
<h4 style="text-align: justify;">RAM</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">RAM means the test of <strong>Random Access Memory</strong> of the device. This memory is allocated to different applications to run in realtime.</p>
<h4 style="text-align: justify;">IO (Storage I/O, Database I/O)</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">IO means the tests of <strong>Input and Output</strong> of the device. This test the storage speeds and database accessing speeds of the device. In simple words, it is the data transfer speeds of the device.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How to get it?</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Antutu Benchmark is easily available for a wide range of devices. It can be downloaded for any Android device (<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.antutu.ABenchMark&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Play Store</a>), iOS Device (<a href="https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/antutu-benchmark/id803837129?mt=8" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">App Store</a>), as well as Windows Phone (<a href="http://www.windowsphone.com/en-us/store/app/antutu-benchmark/82d5a985-4b31-42a6-9ad0-79521cdafff6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Windows Store</a>). It is free to download on all the listed devices.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">How to use?</h2>
[caption width="200" id="attachment_489" align="alignright"]<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/2015-04-20-20.02.58.png"><img class="wp-image-489 size-medium" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/2015-04-20-20.02.58-200x300.png" alt="Antutu Test" width="200" height="300"/></a> Antutu Test[/caption]
<p style="text-align: justify;">You can download for your device from the above download links.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Install Antutu Benchmark on your device.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Launch the app.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It will probably show a Test Button.
And a lot of tests to choose from. You should choose all for a better overall result.
</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Wait a for a few minutes.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">After a process is complete, you will see the final score of your device.</p>
