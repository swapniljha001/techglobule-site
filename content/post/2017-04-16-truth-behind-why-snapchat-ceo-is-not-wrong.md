<h2 style="text-align: center;">Approaching the situation</h2>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Now let's see what's really going on here, one step at a time.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">What is SnapChat?</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">SnapChat is a fun messaging app. You can capture a photo or brief video with it, then add a caption or doodle or filter/lens over the top, and send the <strong>snap</strong> to a friend or set it as your SnapChat <strong>story</strong>, a 24-hour collection of all your snaps that is broadcasted to the world or just your followers.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">But these are merely part of the many features within SnapChat. Yes, at its core, SnapChat is used to send photos and videos to friends. Your friends can view snaps for up to 10 seconds, and then the snaps disappear. While that's all fine and dandy, many people are still left stumped by SnapChat.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Apart from this unique to its platform media sharing, you can just use it for regular chat purposes as well.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">So basically, <em><strong>IT IS A FREE CAMERA APP WITH MESSAGING</strong></em>.</p>
<h4 style="text-align: center;">Where to get it?</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It is available for <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.snapchat.android&amp;hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Android</a> and <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/in/app/snapchat/id447188370?mt=8" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">iPhones</a> as well.
