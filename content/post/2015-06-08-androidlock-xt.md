<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, we will get our iDevice users attain Pattern Lock, the <a href="/category/android/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Android</a> feature, they always longed for.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">What is AndroidLock XT?</h2>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/androidlock-xt.jpg"><img class="alignright wp-image-782 size-medium" src="/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/androidlock-xt-300x169.jpg" alt="AndroidLock XT Logo" width="300" height="169" /></a>
<p style="text-align: justify;">AndroidLock XT is an enhanced version of an older tweak <strong>Android LockScreen</strong> which was meant for earlier iOS versions (below iOS 4).</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It comes pre-installed with new patterns themes and offers various customizations specially made for Security conscious iDevice users, and stylish iDevice users like me as well. ;)</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">What can it do?</h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">AndroidLock XT features a fully customizable pattern lock screen.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It is one of the best lock screen alternative that you should use for Jaibroken iOS 7.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It also comes with maximum unlock attempts limit and also supports emergency calls just like the stock Lock screen.</p>
<h3 style="text-align: center;">Special Features</h3>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
	<li>6 Different themes.</li>
	<li>Customizable appearance and themes.</li>
	<li>Enable/Disable switch.</li>
	<li>Max attempts limit.</li>
	<li>Emergency call.</li>
	<li>Option for asking pattern only after set time.</li>
	<li>iCaughtU support (only iCaughtU PRO, no fake mode).</li>
	<li>It is compatible with a variety of Lock Screen tweaks, for e.g. LockCalendar, LockInfo, BuddyLock, Cydgets, and RecognizeMe Biometric Lock.</li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 1.5;">Option for no pattern if connected to WiFi.</span></li>
	<li><span style="line-height: 1.5;">Option for no pattern if connected to Bluetooth.</span></li>
</ul>
