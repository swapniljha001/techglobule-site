<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, we are unboxing the Micromax YU Yuphoria.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">YU Yuphoria was released in the May of 2015 as a to the YU Yureka which was released earlier in January 2015 by YU, a subsidiary of Micromax. YU Yureka was the first phone released under the YU tag. At the price point of Rs 9k, Yureka provided many specs which were only to be found in flag-ship phones. Yuphoria, released after a few months is kind of a down scaled version of the Yureka but have its own strengths to boast on.</p>
