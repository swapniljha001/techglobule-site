<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
Today we are providing a list of some great tweaks we found this month.
<h2 style="text-align: center;">List of Cydia Tweaks we found in April 2015</h2>
<ul>
	<li>
<h4>Activator</h4>
It is a Toggle system.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.
<a href="/2015/04/activator-tweak/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more...</a></li>
	<li>
<h4>Auxo 3</h4>
It merges the App Switcher and the Control Center.
It costs <strong>2.99 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
	<li>
<h4>CCHide</h4>
It hides specified sections of Control Center.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>CCSettings</h4>
It customizes toggles in Control Center.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>F.lux</h4>
It reduces the white glare of the mobile screen.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on Cydia/Telesphoreo Repo (the default repository).</li>
	<li>
<h4>iCleaner</h4>
It helps saves memory by cleaning waste data.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on ModMyi.com Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>iWidgets</h4>
It is the widget controller of iOS.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on ModMyi.com Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>LastApp</h4>
It is shortcut to Last App.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>LocalIAPStore</h4>
This tweak is used to buy in - app purchases for free.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on In-AppStore.com Cydia Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>SBFlip</h4>
This tweak is used to enjoy iPhone 6 like rotation in any device.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
</ul>
We will discuss these tweaks one by one in future posts.
