<h1 style="text-align: center;">Hi Guys,</h1>
<p style="text-align: justify;">We found and tested this bunch of great and awesome tweaks this month.</p>
<h2 style="text-align: center;">List of Cydia Tweaks we found in June 2015</h2>
<ul>
	<li>
<h4>AndroidLock XT</h4>
This will help you get Pattern Lock for your iDevice.
It costs <strong>1.99 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on ModMyi.com Repo.
<a href="/2015/06/androidlock-xt/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more...</a></li>
	<li>
<h4 style="text-align: justify;">Browser Changer</h4>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It helps a user to change his default Browser.
It costs <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.
<a href="/2015/05/browser-chooser/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more...</a></p>
</li>
	<li style="text-align: justify;">
<h4>BrowserChooser</h4>
As the name suggests, it will allow you to choose from installed browsers whenever you click a link.
It costs <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.
<a href="/2015/05/browser-chooser/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more...</a></li>
</ul>
<ul style="text-align: justify;">
	<li>
<h4>IntelliScreen X</h4>
It will merge Notification Center and Lock Screen in a beautiful manner.
It costs <strong>9.99 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on ModMyi.com Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>SevenClock</h4>
It is a collection of cool widgets featuring weather and clock. It gives an Android feel to the iOS.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on ModMyi.com Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>TimeCode</h4>
This changes your pass code to the current time (of your iOS Device). More secure than traditional.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>WhatsApp Web Enabler</h4>
It adds an option WhatsApp Web to the official WhatsApp App.
It costs <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>WiFi Booster</h4>
It is used to Boost an iDevice's WiFi Capabilities.
It costs <strong>1.99 USD</strong>.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>WiFi Passwords</h4>
This app will store Passwords to any WiFi network you will connect to.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on BigBoss Repo.</li>
	<li>
<h4>WinterBoard</h4>
It is the theme controller of Jailbroken iOS.
It is <strong>Free</strong> of cost.
It is easily available on Cydia/Telesphoreo Repo (the default repository).</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: justify;">We will discuss these tweaks one by one in future posts.</p>
